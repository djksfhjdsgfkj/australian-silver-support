!   Phishing

A fake email or message designed to trick you into giving away personal information. Phishing often uses urgency, threats or fake links.

🔒   HTTPS

A secure website connection that helps protect information you send online. HTTPS protects data travelling between your browser and the website, but it does not prove the website is genuine.

⚠   Malware

Harmful software that can damage a device or steal information. Avoid unexpected downloads and keep software updated.

★   Strong Password

A long, unique password that is difficult for another person to guess. Use different passwords for important accounts.

2   Two-Factor Authentication

An extra security step that asks for a second proof of identity when signing in. A code sent to a trusted device is one common second factor.

⌁   Public Wi-Fi

A shared wireless network in public places that may be less secure than your home network. Avoid sensitive banking on unknown public Wi-Fi.
Tip: Keep definitions short, use familiar words and avoid unnecessary technical language.
