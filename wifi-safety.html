Using Public Wi-Fi Safely

Public Wi-Fi can be useful, but unfamiliar networks are not the best place for sensitive tasks.

STEP 1 — READ

Safer Wi-Fi habits

  • Avoid banking on unknown public Wi-Fi.
  • Use mobile data for sensitive tasks when possible.
  • Do not automatically connect to unfamiliar networks.
  • Use a connection you trust for passwords or payments.
