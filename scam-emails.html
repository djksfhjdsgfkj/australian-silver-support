Spotting Scam Emails

Scam emails can look convincing. Stop and check before you click.

STEP 1 — READ

Key tips to remember

  • Urgency or fear is a common scam warning sign.
  • Do not trust unexpected links or attachments.
  • Never give passwords or security codes through an unexpected message.
  • Verify the sender using contact details you already trust.
